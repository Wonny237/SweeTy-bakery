Our Story

From one home oven to your celebration table.

Golden Crust started in a home kitchen with a single wood-fired oven and a habit of sharing sourdough with the neighbours. Ten years on, we still mix every dough by hand, laminate every croissant fresh at dawn, and taste every batch before it leaves the kitchen.

Everything here is built the same way: real butter, real fruit, real time to prove and rest — and a menu small enough that we can vouch for every single item on it.

  • Family-run kitchen, open since 2014
  • Fresh bakes twice daily, morning & late afternoon
  • Made-to-order party & celebration cakes
